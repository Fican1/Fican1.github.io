      <p class="project-kicker">Research infrastructure · Lead developer</p>
      <h3>Analog EDA software stack</h3>
      <p>A research software stack that connects a server-side optimizer, an approximately 10 GB circuit dataset, a lightweight GUI, and industrial simulators. It integrates Virtuoso, NGSPICE, and Empyrean tools for parameter updates, netlist generation, PVT and Monte Carlo simulation, and result analysis. A lightweight web GUI supports specification input, Pareto candidate selection, and netlist export.</p>
      <div class="project-facts"><span class="project-fact">3 circuit types</span><span class="project-fact">4 tapeouts</span><span class="project-fact">22 topologies</span><span class="project-fact">4 technology nodes</span></div>

    <div class="project-figure">

    </div>
    <div class="project-body">
      <p class="project-kicker">Ongoing team project</p>
      <h3>Self-evolving analog circuit design agent</h3>
      <p>An ongoing framework that routes specification understanding, planning, parameter optimization, and simulation evaluation to specialized agents. Comparison-based GRPO and constrained policy optimization address sparse feedback and competing objectives; trajectory replay and reusable skills support experience reuse, with failure recovery and human intervention.</p>
