I study intelligent optimization for complex decision problems. My work draws on reinforcement learning and evolutionary computation, with an emphasis on multi-objective and constrained optimization. I apply these methods to analog electronic design automation and large-model post-training.

Research Focus

Intelligent Optimization

Learning-based search for expensive problems with competing objectives and hard constraints, using reinforcement learning and evolutionary computation.

Analog EDA

Circuit sizing, behavior-centric representation, PVT-robust design, and industrial EDA integration.

Large-Model Post-Training

Comparison feedback, Pareto-front improvement, preference optimization, and constrained policy learning.
